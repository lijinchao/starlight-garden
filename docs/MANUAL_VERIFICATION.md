# Manual verification — the play loop

Automated checks keep the structural contract and the suites green. They cannot say whether the garden is fun to walk, so that is recorded here, by hand.

## Target

The player the iteration is for: someone who opens the build without being told what to do.

## Task

Launch the exported build from the current worktree, plant the first seeds, follow the breeze path to the next area, and stop at the first moment of confusion or boredom.

## Observation

Date, build, and what happened — friction, confusion, and abandonment, not only success. "It worked" is not an observation; "I could not tell the breeze path was a path" is.

## Decision

What the observation changes, and which automated check now carries the lesson. A journey that lives only in this section has already started decaying.
